How it works

Three decisions, and a folder that installs.

Everything comes from the kit you picked. We read each one's published defaults, and one knob writes into every enabled kit in its own vocabulary — we never invent a token.

  1. Pick a stack.

    Four bands make one: behaviour, an engine, tokens and components. A kit fills some and leans on the rest — shadcn brings the Radix behaviour and its own tokens, and sits on Tailwind's engine.

    And it says what you have to be running: of the kits name no framework at all.

  2. Turn a handful of knobs.

    Twenty roles, no more — a brand colour, a corner radius, the family your body text is set in. One value lands in each kit's own name for it: --primary, --bs-primary, --mantine-primary-color-filled, colorPrimary.

    A length keeps its ratio. A shadow keeps its strength. Their arithmetic, your value.

  3. Install the result.

    A folder with the files each kit expects, plus a manifest naming everything that could not be set and why. No accounts, no sign-up, nothing to keep paying for.

    Or compare six side by side first — the same values in six vocabularies, one click a card.
