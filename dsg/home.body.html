Read, never guessed

Four bands make a stack. Some kits are all four, some are one.

Behaviour, an engine, tokens, components. Most kits fill some and lean on the rest, which is why a combination is often the answer rather than a single name: shadcn brings the behaviour and the tokens and sits on Tailwind's engine. Every count here came out of the kit's own package at build time.
